### Why I am taking MMAE 497

I am taking MMAE 497 as a 2-credit Special Topics course to continue the mechanical design and CAD development of my 6-DOF robotic arm project. The course will allow me to receive academic credit for structured engineering work on the project while working independently under faculty oversight.

The project is particularly appropriate for the course because a major portion of the work involves mechanical engineering, parametric CAD, mechanical design, and design validation. This also directly relates to my Engineering Graphics and CAD minor, and the 2 credits will count toward the requirements for that minor.

### Planned Work

For this semester, I plan to focus primarily on the mechanical/CAD portion of the project. My main objectives are to:

* Develop a parametric CAD skeleton of the 6-DOF robotic arm.
* Establish the joint axes, link geometry, motor locations, bearing locations, and other major mechanical interfaces.
* Develop the J1 (base) subsystem in greater detail, including its motor mounting, belt transmission, bearings, and structural components.
* Develop preliminary/conceptual CAD for the remaining joints.
* Use URDF/Gazebo to validate the basic kinematic structure and motion of the arm, beginning with J1–J3.
* Potentially 3D print selected components when physical prototyping would be useful for validating fit, assembly, clearance, or mechanical concepts.

The broader robotic arm project has longer-term goals involving controls, trajectory optimization, data collection, learning, vision, and autonomy. However, those are future extensions of the project rather than the primary scope of the 2-credit MMAE 497 work.
