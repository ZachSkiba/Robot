**Goal:** Turn your work into lasting external signal that compounds over time, even when you’re busy with school or jobs.

---

### Phase 11 Outcomes

- You’re seen as someone who teaches and leads  
- The project continues to generate value passively  
- Strong references + reputation  

---

#### STEP 11.2 - Teaching and Sharing

**Options (pick 1–2)**

# Phase 13 - Real-World Adoption, Proof & Exit Options

**Duration:** Optional / opportunistic  
**Goal:** Move from “personal platform” to external adoption, validation, or transition — without locking yourselves into one path.  

This phase is about closing the loop with the real world.

---

## Phase 13 Outcomes

- External users or stakeholders  
- Real constraints outside your control  
- Strong third-party validation  
- Clean “exit points” into jobs, research, or startups  

---

## Step-by-Step Plan

### STEP 13.1 — Choose an External Context (Pick ONE)

You deliberately place the system into a real context:

- Research lab (assistive robotics, manipulation)
- Industry demo (automation / QA / inspection)
- Education (teaching platform, robotics club)
- Startup concept (narrow, focused use case)

You’re not committing forever — just testing fit.

---

### STEP 13.2 — Adapt to External Constraints

**Together**  
Handle constraints you didn’t choose:  

- Limited time  
- Different hardware  
- Messy environments  
- Non-technical users  

This is where maturity shows.

---

### STEP 13.3 — External Evaluation

**Zach (DS)**  
- Measure performance under new conditions  
- Compare against baselines  
- Quantify degradation & recovery  

**Nicholas (MechE)**  
- Adapt mechanics to new constraints  
- Improve robustness  
- Handle real-world abuse cases  

**Deliverable:** External performance summary.

---

### STEP 13.4 — Decide the Exit Path

Explicitly choose one:

- Research continuation (paper / thesis)
- Job leverage (portfolio + interviews)
- Startup pivot (very narrow scope)
- Archive & move on (with confidence)

Choosing to stop is also success.

---

### STEP 13.5 — Final Signal

Produce one clean artifact:

- Case study  
- Demo for an external audience  
- Short write-up: problem → system → results → lessons
